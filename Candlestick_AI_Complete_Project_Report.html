Chapter 1: Executive Summary

Candlestick AI is an AI-powered financial technology platform engineered for quantitative market data visualization, technical indicator calculation, and simulated paper trading on Indian equities (NSE & BSE).

The platform pairs high-speed interactive SVG financial charting with a modular market-data provider service, mathematical indicator algorithms (SMA, EMA, Wilder's RSI, Bollinger Bands), a virtual portfolio simulator pre-funded with ₹10,00,000 (10 Lakhs INR), and a server-side AI analyst grounded in live exchange data via Google's Gemini models.

Chapter 2: Implemented Capabilities & Verified Results

  • Universal Indian Equity Search: Intelligent resolution supporting company names (e.g. Fineotex → FCL.NS, Reliance → RELIANCE.NS) and autocomplete dropdown.
  • Interactive Candlestick Chart: Real-time and 15-minute delayed daily candles, Volume histograms, SMA 20/50, Bollinger Bands, and RSI/MACD sub-charts.
  • Portfolio Performance Engine: Visual progress bar benchmarking equity against the initial ₹10,00,000 baseline with asset allocation breakdown (Liquid Cash vs. Equities).
  • Server-Side AI Proxy: Grounded responses citing verified numbers with SEBI risk disclosures, preventing client-side secret exposure.
